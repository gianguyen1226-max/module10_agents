## Vietnamese Restaurant Assistant

My assistant is a chatbot for a Vietnamese restaurant that assist customers in nagviating the menu prices. It utilizes a separate tool to find prices for items such as pho, banh mi, and spring rolls. The chatbot is a mock assistant because the menu and prices are similar to the prices around the area. Questions that require more in depth than the menu such as allergies or placing orders will be passed to an employee. Those situations will require an acutal person to assist the customer.
